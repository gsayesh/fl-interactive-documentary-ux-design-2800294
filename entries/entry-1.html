Pandavleni Caves

1st c. BCE – 3rd c. CE · Trirashmi hill

On screen now
Summary
Explore
Sources

Twenty-four Buddhist caves cut into Trirashmi hill between the 1st century BCE and the 3rd century CE. Their donor inscriptions name Satavahana kings and their Kshatrapa rivals, making the caves the best-dated record of who ruled Nashik, and when.

State flow
Interaction rules
Never interruptThe film pauses only on a deliberate tap, never by itself. Chips are invitations with a visible timer.
Keep the film in viewA docked mini-player with the paused frame and timestamp keeps the sense of place while reading.
Depth on demandSummary › Explore › Sources. Three levels, one control, breadcrumb always visible.
One-tap returnA fixed “Resume at m:ss” button is the only primary action in reading mode. Resume continues playback.
Links go both waysPlaces, people and sources carry ▶ timestamps that jump the documentary to the matching shot.
Works without the chipsScrub-bar ticks, the topic list and the chapters sheet give the same content with auto-pause off.

What is interactive
Chip timer8-second countdown ring; ignored chips leave on their own and nothing changes.
Auto-pause toggleOff = no chips. Scrub ticks, topic rows and the chapters sheet still open every topic.
Timestamps jump the film▶ 5:04 on a place or person seeks the documentary and shows a “Jumped to” toast.
Resume at the exact frameOne fixed primary button; the paused time is kept while you read and explore.
Chapters sheetWatched · Watching · Jump per era, progress bars and read counts, from the ≡ icon.
